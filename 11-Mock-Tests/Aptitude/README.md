# Aptitude Mocks

**Weeks 9–10 + the aptitude section of the Week 12 full-length mocks** · [← Mock Tests](../README.md)

## Format
30 questions in 35 minutes (about 70 seconds each): 15 quantitative, 10 logical reasoning, 5 verbal.

## 📅 Tasks
- [ ] Week 9 — Mock 1: topics from weeks 1–9 → `mock-01.md`, error review in `mock-01-review.md`
- [ ] Week 10 — Mock 2: all topics, mixed and harder → `mock-02.md`, error review in `mock-02-review.md`
- [ ] Week 12 — the aptitude section of each full-length mock → `full-01.md`, `full-02.md`, `full-03.md`

## 💡 Test Strategy
- First pass: answer everything you can solve in under a minute; mark the rest
- Second pass: go back to the marked questions
- Never spend more than 2 minutes on one question
- Check whether wrong answers carry negative marks before you guess
