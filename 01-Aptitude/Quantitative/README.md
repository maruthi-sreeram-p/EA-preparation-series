# Quantitative Aptitude

**Weeks 1–6** · 30 min a day · 16 topics · [← Aptitude](../README.md)

Each week: learn the concepts and shortcuts, write the notes file, then practise about 100 questions (15–20 a day).

## Week 1 — Number System, HCF & LCM, Simplification
- [ ] Number system: types of numbers, divisibility rules, unit digit of powers, remainders, number of factors → `01-number-system.md`
- [ ] HCF & LCM: prime factorisation and division methods, HCF × LCM = product of two numbers, HCF/LCM of fractions → `02-hcf-lcm.md`
- [ ] Simplification: BODMAS, approximation, squares up to 30 and cubes up to 20, surds and indices → `03-simplification.md`
- [ ] Practice: ~100 questions

## Week 2 — Percentages, Profit & Loss
- [ ] Percentages: fraction ↔ percentage table (1/2 to 1/20), percentage change, successive changes, population and depreciation → `04-percentages.md`
- [ ] Profit & loss: CP, SP, MP, discount, successive discounts, false weights, profit % on CP → `05-profit-and-loss.md`
- [ ] Practice: ~100 questions

## Week 3 — Ratio & Proportion, Averages, Ages
- [ ] Ratio & proportion: combining ratios, proportionals, partnership, mixtures & alligation → `06-ratio-proportion.md`
- [ ] Averages: simple and weighted averages; adding, removing and replacing members → `07-averages.md`
- [ ] Ages: present/past/future age equations, ratio-based age problems → `08-ages.md`
- [ ] Practice: ~100 questions

## Week 4 — Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns
- [ ] Time & work: total-work (LCM) method, efficiency, work and wages, working on alternate days → `09-time-and-work.md`
- [ ] Pipes & cisterns: inlet vs outlet, leaks, pipes opened and closed at different times → `10-pipes-and-cisterns.md`
- [ ] Practice: ~100 questions

## Week 5 — Speed & Distance, Trains, Boats & Streams
- [ ] Speed & distance: km/h → m/s (× 5/18), average speed, relative speed → `11-speed-distance.md`
- [ ] Trains: crossing a pole, a platform, and another train (same and opposite directions) → `12-trains.md`
- [ ] Boats & streams: downstream = boat + stream, upstream = boat − stream → `13-boats-and-streams.md`
- [ ] Practice: ~100 questions

## Week 6 — Interest, Permutations & Combinations, Probability
- [ ] Simple & compound interest: formulas, CI − SI for 2 and 3 years, half-yearly and quarterly compounding → `14-interest.md`
- [ ] Permutations & combinations: counting principle, nPr, nCr, arranging letters with repeats, circular arrangements → `15-permutations-combinations.md`
- [ ] Probability: coins, dice, cards, balls in a bag, "at least one" questions → `16-probability.md`
